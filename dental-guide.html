Daily care

Brush carefully twice a day with fluoride toothpaste and clean between your teeth regularly. A dentist or oral hygienist can show you techniques suited to your mouth.

02

Dental visits

Regular examinations can help identify changes early. The timing of check-ups differs from person to person, so ask the practice what interval is suitable for you.

03

When to call

Contact a dental professional when you have persistent pain, swelling, bleeding, a broken tooth or a change that concerns you. Severe swelling, trauma or trouble breathing may need urgent medical attention.

04

Cosmetic care

Whitening, shade matching and dental jewellery should begin with an oral assessment. Ask about suitability, expected care, possible risks and costs before treatment.
